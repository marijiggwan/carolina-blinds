Is Hunter Douglas Expensive? The Answer Changed.

For years, the best-known name in window coverings cost noticeably more than brands you'd never heard of. In 2026, that price gap has largely closed — but most homeowners don't know it yet. Here's what changed, and what it means for your project.

The Short Answer

Ask ten homeowners whether Hunter Douglas is expensive, and most will say yes — because for a long time, it was true. Hunter Douglas priced meaningfully above other custom window treatment brands, and lower-cost alternatives could save a typical project thousands of dollars.

That's not what we see in our quotes anymore. As a Western North Carolina dealer writing estimates every week across multiple brands, we've watched the gap compress. Manufacturers across the industry have raised prices, and in the quotes we prepare today, Hunter Douglas and comparable custom brands frequently land at or near the same number.

Which raises the question every homeowner should be asking in 2026: if the price is about the same — why settle for a brand you've never heard of?

Where the "Hunter Douglas Is Expensive" Idea Came From

The reputation was earned honestly. Hunter Douglas invented entire product categories — the Duette® honeycomb shade, Silhouette® window shadings, Luminette® privacy sheers — and invested heavily in research, engineering, and a limited lifetime warranty. Premium products, premium prices.

Other manufacturers built their business on the other side of that equation: engineer a similar-looking product to sell at a significantly lower price point, and let the savings do the selling. For years, that strategy worked, and the price gap between Hunter Douglas and the value brands was real and substantial. Homeowners heard it from friends, read it on forums, and filed it away: Hunter Douglas = the expensive one.

Consumer memory is durable. Prices aren't.

What Changed

Over the past several years, every manufacturer in the industry has taken repeated price increases — materials, components, freight, labor. But the increases haven't landed evenly. The value brands, starting from lower price points, have climbed steeply. The result, window by window and quote by quote: the gap has narrowed to the point where it often barely exists.

We see it in our own estimate work. When we quote a comparable product from Hunter Douglas alongside a lesser-known brand, the numbers routinely come back closer than customers expect — sometimes within a few percent, sometimes with Hunter Douglas ahead by far less than the "expensive brand" reputation would suggest.

To be clear: pricing depends on the specific product line, fabric, size, and options, and there are still configurations where one brand or another wins on price. That's exactly why we quote them side by side. But the blanket assumption — that choosing Hunter Douglas means paying a big premium — no longer holds up against the actual numbers.

If the Price Is the Same, What Are You Really Choosing?

When the price gap was wide, choosing a value brand was a rational trade: give up some innovation and warranty strength, save real money. When the prices converge, the trade disappears — and the comparison shifts to what you actually get:

Proprietary designs. Silhouette®, Duette®, Luminette®, Pirouette® — these aren't generic categories; they're Hunter Douglas inventions you can't buy under another label.

PowerView® motorization. The most mature motorization and smart-home platform in the industry, with integration across major home automation systems.

Warranty and parts. A limited lifetime warranty backed by a manufacturer that's been making window coverings for over 75 years — and as an authorized service provider, we can order OEM parts directly when you need them.

Local accountability. Whichever brand you choose, at Carolina Blinds it's one place, one team, start to finish: our own employees handle your consultation, measurement, installation, and service — with free in-home service for the first year.

We also carry Alta Window Fashions and Eclipse, and there are projects where those lines are genuinely the right answer. Our job isn't to sell you the biggest name — it's to show you real numbers for your actual windows and let you decide.
