Built for Fairview Homes

Big Views, Western Sun, Tall Great Rooms — Handled

Fairview homes were built for the view — long sightlines across the Cane Creek Valley toward the Craggies and Hickory Nut Gorge. The challenge is enjoying that view without living in glare, heat, and faded furniture. Standard off-the-shelf coverings force a bad trade: block the sun, lose the view.

This is where custom solutions earn their keep. Solar shades cut glare and UV while remaining nearly transparent from inside — the view stays, the heat doesn’t. Top-down/bottom-up shades manage privacy while leaving sightlines untouched. And for the tall great-room windows and gable glass common in Fairview’s ridgeline and newer construction homes, Hunter Douglas PowerView® motorization means every shade moves together at the touch of a button or on an automated schedule. No ladders, no cords, no compromise.

During your free consultation, we’ll walk your home, assess each window’s orientation and height, and recommend solutions that fit both the architecture and the way you actually live.
